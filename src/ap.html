Using these courses

Each course runs 36 weeks, unit by unit, with a unit test at the end of each unit and a full-length practice exam in week 33. Tests and exams print as a student copy with an answer sheet and a separate answer key with scoring guidelines.

Results are raw points by section and unit. They are practice, not AP scores, and no score conversion is given. These are independent courses, not authorized AP courses, and they do not award credit.
