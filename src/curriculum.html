Make the plan fit the learner.

Use the opening prerequisites to find a starting point. Inside each unit, introduce and model a small idea, practise with support, apply it independently, and revisit earlier work. Reserve time every week for feedback and repair; the 36 weeks include review and checks. Slow down or extend a unit when the evidence calls for it.
