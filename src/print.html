What these booklets are worth

Use them for pacing, endurance, and answer-sheet practice. The question banks are generated from a limited set of templates, so accuracy on a form runs well above what the same student would score on an official test. Time yourself honestly, and treat the percentage as practice feedback, not a score prediction.
