    The school year

    Using nightly readings

    Read aloud, together, or independently as indicated. Use the suggested minutes for reading, rereading, and conversation; actual pace varies. A short poem can reward several readings. Stop at a useful point and return when needed.

    The school year has 36 weeks of five nights. The date calendar is written one year at a time. In each year it covers, a level has a reading for every day, and a date’s reading does not repeat for three years. Discussion questions invite supported interpretations; discussion help is optional and stays out of student printouts and downloads. Every text is public domain in the United States and carries its source and edition.
