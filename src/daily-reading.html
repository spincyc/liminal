Using daily reading

Read aloud, together, or independently as indicated. Use the suggested minutes for reading, rereading, and conversation; actual pace varies. A short poem can reward several readings. Stop at a useful point and return when needed.

Discussion questions invite supported interpretations. Facilitator notes are optional and stay out of student printouts and downloads. Literary selections are public domain in the United States; each reading carries its source and edition.
