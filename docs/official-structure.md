# Official Test Structure and Content Map

Verified against primary official sources on 2026-07-29. This document records
the public test blueprints used to organize independent original practice
content; it does not reproduce test questions.

## SAT

The digital SAT has two sections:

- **Reading and Writing:** 64 minutes. Each short passage or passage pair has
  one discrete question.
- **Math:** 70 minutes. Questions include multiple-choice and
  student-produced-response formats. Calculator use is permitted throughout
  the digital Math section using an approved calculator or the embedded Desmos
  calculator.

### SAT Reading and Writing domains

| Domain | Skills represented in this platform |
| --- | --- |
| Information and Ideas | Central Ideas and Details; Inferences; Command of Evidence (textual and quantitative) |
| Craft and Structure | Words in Context; Text Structure and Purpose; Cross-Text Connections |
| Expression of Ideas | Rhetorical Synthesis; Transitions |
| Standard English Conventions | Boundaries; Form, Structure, and Sense |

### SAT Math domains

| Domain | Official approximate share | Skills represented in this platform |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Algebra | 35% | Linear equations; linear functions; two-variable equations; systems; inequalities |
| Advanced Math | 35% | Equivalent expressions; nonlinear equations and systems; nonlinear functions |
| Problem-Solving and Data Analysis | 15% | Ratios/rates/units; percentages; one- and two-variable data; probability; inference; study design |
| Geometry and Trigonometry | 15% | Area/volume; lines/angles/triangles; right-triangle trigonometry; circles |

The practice app uses difficulty labels for transparent study filtering. It
does not claim to implement College Board's multistage adaptive routing or
scaled scoring.

## ACT

The enhanced ACT has three required multiple-choice sections and two optional
sections:

| Section | Current format | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English | 50 questions, 35 minutes | Required |
| Mathematics | 45 questions, 50 minutes | Required |
| Reading | 36 questions, 40 minutes | Required |
| Science | 40 questions, 40 minutes | Optional |
| Writing | 1 essay, 40 minutes | Optional |

The current Composite score is based on English, Mathematics, and Reading.
Science receives a separate section score and contributes to a STEM score when
taken. Writing receives a separate score and contributes to an ELA score when
taken.

### ACT reporting categories

| Section | Domains used by this platform |
| --- | --- |
| English | Production of Writing; Knowledge of Language; Conventions of Standard English |
| Mathematics | Preparing for Higher Math (Number and Quantity, Algebra, Functions, Geometry, Statistics and Probability); Integrating Essential Skills; Modeling |
| Reading | Key Ideas and Details; Craft and Structure; Integration of Knowledge and Ideas |
| Science | Interpretation of Data; Scientific Investigation; Evaluation of Models, Inferences, and Experimental Results |
| Writing | Ideas and Analysis; Development and Support; Organization; Language Use and Conventions |

ACT publicly describes Modeling as integrated across Mathematics rather than a
separate mutually exclusive bucket. In this bank, each Math item has one primary
domain and may also carry `modeling` as a format/tag. Writing prompts are
classified by their main planning challenge while every prompt's guide covers
all four rubric domains.
