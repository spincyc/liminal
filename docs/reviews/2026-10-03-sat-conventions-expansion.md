## Independent review and repairs

Each additional design had its own cold author agent. Separate reviewers solved
at least three distinct scenes per affected family before seeing keys, then
inspected source, alternatives and teaching material. The final admission
manifest contains 51 accepted records and 204 displayed-sample answers, bound
to current versions and source fingerprints. It identifies each author and
reviewer. These are finite independent agent reviews, not human approval or
empirical difficulty calibration.

Reviewers were `/root/expand_rw_conventions/review_existing_boundaries`,
`review_existing_forms`, and `review_new_1` through `review_new_9` under the same
coordinator. The final manifest names the reviewer whose accepted assessment
applies; an earlier rejection is not an acceptance.

The work also repairs inherited questions:

- Tallis choir and Mary Rose contexts now share canonical scene identities.
  Two regressions check those identities and the former Tallis collision seed.
- Existing nested-genitive ownership and dangling-modifier grids move from
  Hard to Medium because their direct actor/count decisions were routine.
- New infinitive time/voice, joint/separate possession and reflexive counterpart
  designs remain Medium after review. Additional Hard designs replace the
  capacity; their rubric scores were not increased to preserve the original
  proposal.
- Four quotation-comma scenes that allowed integrated alternatives were
  rewritten, and the unreachable appositive teaching branch was removed.
  All twelve scenes received renewed review.
- Essential-clause context now consistently tests restrictive subset selection.
  Nine retained and three new scenes establish an excluded group that makes
  the comma reading false. All twelve final scenes received cold review.
- Possessive-count, modifier, relative-event-time and pronoun-comparison
  explanations were corrected. Shared-role agreement's trap rating was reduced.

Source attribution and exception scope share a punctuation-choice structure
with existing attachment designs. Their distinctions lie in the contextual
reasoning, not a newly claimed punctuation construction. Reviewers explicitly
assessed that relationship.

The exception-scope draft was repeatedly rejected when a stated universal,
counterexample or group difference bypassed its proposed reasoning. The final
pool retains two supported core scenes and six substantive replacements. The
same final reviewer solved twelve new order variants before keys and verified
the four unchanged core variants against prior blind answers. All eight
shortest solution routes supported provisional Hard 9. A 154-condition source
audit and a separate 6,000-draw check passed. One explanation was corrected to
say two substances were gone by pouring without inventing their relative
removal order; the question, choices and key did not change.

Six cold scene-author subtasks supplied replacement candidates within that one
design. Five supplied accepted scenes; an optical theatre proposal was
withdrawn for a shortcut, and the original design author supplied the final
sapling scene. Rejected drafts do not count as admitted designs or scenes.

## Source impact and verification

The coordinator compared 300 seeds per original family against the baseline
and reread three final-source samples from each of six changed original
families. Independent reviewers assessed affected siblings. Appending the final
exception design preserved the accepted Boundaries prefix and 100 draws for
each of its 22 prior siblings. Its two retained core scenes and their four
reviewed order variants were unchanged.

306,000 draws. The two scene-identity regressions and whitespace check pass.

are repaired, but this does not establish that every possible semantic topic
overlap elsewhere in the corpus has been eliminated.
