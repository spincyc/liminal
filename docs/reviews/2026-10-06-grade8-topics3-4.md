## Finding resolved before acceptance

`g8-t3-compare-function-rates:review-a`,
`g8-t3-compare-function-rates:review-b`, and
`g8-t3-compare-function-rates:review-c` were initially rejected for prompt
sufficiency. The original prompt said “Function B is shown in the table” and asked which function
“has the greater rate of change.” The key attributes an unqualified rate to
B. B is not stated to be linear, and the comparison is not restricted to
average rates over the displayed intervals.

For `review-a`, B's displayed points are `(0,1)`, `(2,11)`, and `(4,21)`.
Their average rates are 5, so the intended comparison with A's rate 2 is
correct over those intervals. However, every function
`B(x) = 5x + 1 + Kx(x − 2)(x − 4)` fits the same table, with behavior away
from those inputs unconstrained. A finite table cannot establish the implied
global constant rate without a linearity or domain assumption. Reading the
table as the complete finite domain makes the intended key defensible, but
the prompt should state its intended scope.

The author adopted the recommended minimal change: “Function B is linear and
is shown in the table.” The reviewer re-evaluated all three revised prompts:
linearity now makes the constant rates 5, 2, and 9 uniquely determined, and
the original comparisons and explanations are valid. Final replay verified
exactly this replacement in those three prompts and no other display or key
changes across all 84 samples. All three revised samples are accepted.

The original Topic 3 SHA-256 was
`6afdd116b2668e931757fad3afa6cf0229ab9603af44c397bd61542621f286a3`;
the final accepted fingerprint appears in the source table above. The
affected source is `grade8-topic3.js`, lines 78–85. This reviewer made no
production edits. Retaining this finding explains why explicit linearity
belongs in the prompt rather than being inferred from a few table values.

## Limits

This is an independent agent review of three deterministic samples per
template, not an exhaustive seed search, human editorial approval, empirical
difficulty measurement, or curriculum certification. For example, these
three `vertical-line-points` draws all produced nonfunctions and these three
`model-prediction` draws all produced interpolation; other branches are not
covered by this sample review. The reviewer inspected graph coordinates,
line endpoints, scales, and their mathematical meaning in the structured
display data, not rendered browser pages or printed PDFs. Visual clipping,
label overlap, handwriting space, pagination, and answer exclusion need
separate rendering checks. No production files were changed, and no commit
or push was performed by this reviewer.
