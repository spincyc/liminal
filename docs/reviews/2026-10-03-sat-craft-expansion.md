# Craft and Structure expansion — original content and sampled independent review

## Scope

Six original question designs were authored by six separate cold child agents. The lane modifies only the three Craft and Structure skill source files. Four additions are provisionally Hard and two are Easy. They add 64 finite original scenes, not 64 independent template designs. No external passage or official question was used.

## Design distinctions

- `wic-separated-context-constraints` (meaning_constraints): determine the sense of a stated expression by tracing its particular referent through separated evidence and consequences; nearby events support a different, tempting sense.
- `wic-latent-relationship` (precision_relational): identify a precise relationship after reconciling membership, time scale, generalization, controlled contrasts, or the separate and joint effects of interventions.
- `tsp-function-evaluation-frame` (function_reframe): explain how a sentence changes the criterion used to evaluate accepted observations, linking an earlier criticism to later evidence.
- `cross-text-different-evaluation-standards` (cross_evidence_standard): locate different evaluative targets in two accounts that accept the same observations, using comparisons and counterfactuals to distinguish plausible competing standards.
- `tsp-function-prediction` (easy_structure_prediction): distinguish an explicit pretrial expectation from a later observation, definition, or recommendation.
- `cross-text-distinct-research-purposes` (easy_cross_procedure): identify each project's directly stated purpose, with distractors that misassign one or both purposes.

## Editorial process

Three independent reviewers first saved 99 blind answers covering three distinct scenes of every existing Craft template before viewing keys. All matched. Adding a new template conservatively invalidates its file siblings' source fingerprints, so these reviewers also assessed the actual source impact rather than refreshing old approvals automatically.

The Structure review identified three inherited overstatements. The concession template now explains how a favorable assessment survives a drawback rather than claiming its final sentence invariably restates the initial claim. View-complication choices and feedback no longer equate evidence against a causal attribution with conclusive refutation. The literary-function trap now covers development and payoff as well as reversal. Existing passage text, stems, scenes, and answer indices were preserved; some response and feedback wording changed.

The initial new Words and paired-text designs had correct, unique keys but contained scenes too direct for their proposed Hard tier. Reviewers withheld approval. Their authors replaced or rewrote the weak scenes, supplied closer competing interpretations, and strengthened the need to combine separated facts. A further pass replaced the transport comparison with a separate-versus-joint intervention problem and revised the marsh comparison to distinguish original community membership from restored ecological relationships. The final scene pools received all-scene feedback and difficulty reassessment. Original draft responses were kept separate and were not used to admit substantively revised final templates.

The final admission bundle contains three distinct-scene blind answers for each current template. It identifies each author, independent reviewer, sample seed, answer, scene, and source file hash. Fresh final reviewers handled revised Words and paired-text samples before seeing their current keys; informed reassessments are separately labeled and never counted as fresh cold solves.

## Verification

Each final Craft skill file passed tools/check-families.js with 3,000 integer seeds and 3,000 runtime-shaped seeds per template: 39 templates and 234,000 final draws in total. This covers structural requirements, passage bounds, distinct scenes/items, answer positions, answer-length tells, word/opening frequency, hub/look-alike/similar-pair strategies, and declared rubric bands. No gate was relaxed. git diff --check passed for the three owned files.

A coordinator snapshot compared 1,000 generated outputs for each of the 33 existing templates. Exactly the three intended Structure repairs changed output. Independently, Words review compared 11,000 existing outputs with no differences; Cross review established exact additive-only source reconstruction and unchanged reviewed records; Structure review compared 3,300 existing draws and found only the documented response/feedback changes. Final source revisions remain isolated to the six added designs and those three repairs.

## Limits

These are sampled agent editorial judgments, not human editorial approval or empirical SAT difficulty calibration. Reviewers read every new finite scene and its feedback, while only the identified distinct samples were solved cold. Some cases sit at the lower edge of provisional Hard, including the effective-string sense, musical-originality and city-independence comparisons, and inherited hive-thresholds item. Finite scene banks can repeat after exhaustion. Final integration and verification are recorded in the [corpus report](2026-10-03-sat-corpus-expansion.md).
