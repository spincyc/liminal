# 8-Week Standard Plan

**The default.** Enough time for genuine skill change without burning out.
Roughly **6-9 hours per week**.

**Prerequisites:** run [Diagnostic routing](diagnostic-routing.md) first, and
read [Drill protocols](drill-protocols.md).

---

## Structure

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I — Foundation** | 1-2 | Diagnose, fix mechanics, learn how items are built |
| **II — Content** | 3-5 | Systematic domain work, weakest first |
| **III — Speed** | 6-7 | Real timing, mixed practice, pacing |
| **IV — Taper** | 8 | Consolidate, rest, arrive fluent |

Four full practice tests: weeks 1, 3, 6, and 7.

---

## Phase I — Foundation (weeks 1-2)

### Week 1

| Day | Work | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Full diagnostic test**, real conditions | 3 h |
| 2 | Classify every miss. Complete [diagnostic routing](diagnostic-routing.md). Build your target list. | 2 h |
| 3 | Read [How questions are built](../04-how-questions-are-built.md) + [Universal answer patterns](../05-universal-answer-patterns.md). 20 mixed items applying them. | 2 h |
| 4 | Read the overview for each section you're testing. 20 items in your weakest domain. | 1.5 h |
| 5 | Grammar block: read [SEC](../sat/reading-writing/04-standard-english-conventions.md) or [ACT Conventions](../act/english/03-conventions-of-standard-english.md). 25 items untimed. | 1.5 h |
| 6 | Missed-items + 20 mixed | 1 h |
| 7 | Rest | — |

### Week 2 — mechanics

**The theme is the five careless-error habits.** Drill at 80% speed all week
while building them.

| Day | Work | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Weak domain 1: read the guide, 20 items untimed writing every step | 1.5 h |
| 2 | Grammar: 30 items, join table and forbidden-comma list | 1.5 h |
| 3 | Weak domain 1: 25 more items | 1.5 h |
| 4 | Formula reference — **handwrite it once**. 20 items applying it. | 1.5 h |
| 5 | Weak domain 2: read the guide, 20 items untimed | 1.5 h |
| 6 | Missed-items + weekly audit | 1 h |
| 7 | Rest | — |

**Also start this week:** if SAT, daily 20-minute
[Desmos practice](../sat/math/06-desmos-playbook.md). If ACT Science, the
[orientation habit](../act/science/00-overview.md), narrated aloud.

## Phase II — Content (weeks 3-5)

The core of the plan. Work through your target domains systematically, weakest
first.

### The per-domain cycle

Each domain gets roughly three sessions:

| Session | Work |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Read the guide. 15 items **untimed**, writing every step. |
| 2 | 20 items **untimed** at Medium. Full review. |
| 3 | 20 items **timed** at target pace. Full review. |

Then move on, and let the Missed mode bring it back on the spacing schedule.

### Week 3

| Day | Work | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Domain A, session 1 | 1.5 h |
| 2 | Domain A, session 2 | 1.5 h |
| 3 | Grammar maintenance: 25 items timed | 1 h |
| 4 | Domain A, session 3 | 1.5 h |
| 5 | Domain B, session 1 | 1.5 h |
| 6 | **Full practice test** | 3 h |
| 7 | Same-day impressions. Rest. | 0.5 h |

### Week 4

| Day | Work | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Test review, item by item. Extract three changes. Re-route. | 2.5 h |
| 2 | Domain B, session 2 | 1.5 h |
| 3 | Domain B, session 3 | 1.5 h |
| 4 | Domain C, session 1 | 1.5 h |
| 5 | Domain C, session 2 | 1.5 h |
| 6 | Missed-items + weekly audit | 1 h |
| 7 | Rest | — |

### Week 5

| Day | Work | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Domain C, session 3 | 1.5 h |
| 2 | Domain D, session 1 | 1.5 h |
| 3 | Domain D, session 2 | 1.5 h |
| 4 | Domain D, session 3 | 1.5 h |
| 5 | **Hard-difficulty drill** across domains A-D, 25 items untimed | 1.5 h |
| 6 | Missed-items + weekly audit | 1 h |
| 7 | Rest | — |

**Note the difficulty progression.** By week 5 you should be working Medium and
Hard, not Easy. If you're still at 60% on Easy in a domain, that domain needs
another cycle before moving on.

---

## Phase III — Speed (weeks 6-7)

Content is in place. Now make it work at real pace.

### Week 6

| Day | Work | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pacing drills: 2 sections at real timing with [checkpoints](../06-timing-and-pacing.md) | 1.5 h |
| 2 | **Mixed-mode** drill, 40 items, real timing | 1.5 h |
| 3 | Weakest remaining domain: 25 items timed | 1.5 h |
| 4 | Compressed: one section at **90% of real time** | 1 h |
| 5 | Missed-items + flagged review | 1 h |
| 6 | **Full practice test** | 3 h |
| 7 | Same-day impressions. Rest. | 0.5 h |

### Week 7

| Day | Work | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Test review. Extract three changes. | 2.5 h |
| 2 | Mixed-mode, 40 items, real timing | 1.5 h |
| 3 | Targeted work on whatever the test exposed | 1.5 h |
| 4 | Compressed practice, one section at 90% | 1 h |
| 5 | Missed-items + reread your rules list | 1 h |
| 6 | **Final full practice test** (7-10 days before the real one) | 3 h |
| 7 | Rest | — |

**Phase III is where mixed practice matters.** Isolated domain drilling doesn't
train *skill identification* — recognizing which method applies. That's a
separate skill and it's why students who drill only by domain plateau.

---

## Phase IV — Taper (week 8)

No new material.

| Day | Work | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Final test review. Extract at most two changes. | 2 h |
| 2 | Light mixed drilling, 30 items, mostly strong skills | 1 h |
| 3 | Reread your rules list. 20 items in your weakest skill. | 1 h |
| 4 | Reread the [cheat sheets](../08-cheat-sheets.md) and your formula reference. 20 easy items. | 1 h |
| 5 | 20 warm-up items. **Logistics check:** ID, calculator batteries, route, Bluebook setup, admission ticket. | 0.5 h |
| 6 | **Rest.** Lay everything out. Sleep early. | — |
| 7 | **Test day** | — |

### The last three days

- No new material
- Light drilling only, mostly in skills you're good at
- Reread your error-log rules list — this is its entire payoff
- Sleep; two nights before matters more than the night before
- No full test the day before

## Adapting the plan

**If you're testing both SAT and ACT:** don't run this twice in parallel. Do the
tests in sequence — full plan for the first, then a
[4-week sprint](4-week-sprint.md) for the second, focused on format differences
rather than content. Most content transfers.

**If you're only weak in one section:** compress Phase II to two weeks and
extend Phase III. Mixed practice and pacing benefit everyone.

**If a domain isn't improving after three sessions:** re-diagnose. A persistent
"content" gap is usually a process problem underneath — you know the material
but set it up wrong. Switch to untimed, write-every-step work.

**If you fall behind:** cut drilling volume, never the review block. Half the
items with full review beats all the items with none.

---

## Weekly audit checklist

Every week, 20 minutes:

- [ ] Count error types. Which dominates?
- [ ] Count misses by skill. What are the top two or three?
- [ ] Reread the rules list. Am I still breaking old rules?
- [ ] Did last week's targets improve? If not, re-diagnose.
- [ ] Adjust next week's targets accordingly
